This browser can't read text from pictures yet

GameSpeak reads text with the browser's own built-in text detector. Your browser doesn't have it, and no open source text-recognition library is in the starred repos, so there is nothing safe to ship in its place yet.

What works today:

  • On iPhone, open the screenshot in Photos, use Live Text and choose Copy All, then paste it in the box below.
  • VoiceOver can also recognize text in images on its own.
  • In desktop Chrome, turning on "Experimental Web Platform features" in chrome://flags can enable the text detector.
